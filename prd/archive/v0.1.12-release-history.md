## What shipped (finish & polish)

From the post-0.1.11 "温故而知新" review (see `plan/plan-0.1.12-review.md`):

- **Composer Shift+Arrow text selection.** A real anchor/caret model: Shift+←/→
  by character, Shift+↑/↓ by line (column-preserving, cell-based so CJK aligns),
  Shift+Home/End to line ends (line-relative in a multiline draft). Typing,
  Backspace/Delete, cut and copy act on the selection; a per-row highlight is
  painted under the selected cells.
- **Theme consistency** from the 0.1.11 terminal-color work: the crosshair and
  scrollbar now follow the theme (a hardcoded white crosshair was invisible on
  Paper); the L###:C### readout follows the cursor when the pointer leaves the
  terminal.
- **Discoverability:** the settings-panel shortcut list is complete and correct
  (it had mislabeled tab-switch as `Ctrl+[ / ]` and omitted theme/settings/
  crosshair/history); the panel shows a live zoom % and closes on Escape.
- **Polish:** tab titles ellipsize instead of clipping mid-glyph; the close
  button has a hover plate and a non-zero shell exit colors the tab title;
  New/Settings header buttons have hover feedback.
- **Paste fix:** the terminal paste-review preview no longer collapses multiline
  clipboard content to one line on macOS/Linux (emit the host control's newline
  form — CRLF on Windows, LF elsewhere).

The runtime was untouched — the review's stability survey found the PTY /
control / resize / paint paths already hardened.
