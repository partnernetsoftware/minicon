## What shipped

`mux` + `harness` subcommands (DeepSeek and opencode-go wire codecs). Scope
and non-goals owned in `prd/PRD_02_31_v0_2_horizon.md`.

## Evidence and its limits

- `./scripts/build.sh test` green under a display server: 517 tests, zero
  failures (host unit suite, alignment, mux black boxes, harness codecs, 28
  GUI black boxes under `xvfb-run`).
- `./scripts/six-cell-qualify.sh`: FAIL 0 / PASS 23 / BLOCKED 18 on the
  repinned `agenterm` — proves the Windows console-agent code, the
  `native-tls` arm of `network-http`, and the macOS process adapter compile
  and link, not that they execute.
- **No runtime evidence at ship time**: six-cell on a non-macOS host links but
  never executes; all four runtime courts were BLOCKED on unconfigured
  runners, and the Apple cells' `test`/`throughput` stages were BLOCKED
  because a Linux kernel answers `Exec format error` to a Mach-O binary.
  Cross-compilation is not execution, and this gap was never claimed closed —
  0.2.0 shipped on cross-compile + static-signing + static-scan evidence
  only.

## Two real defects found and fixed during the release, not glossed over

1. `scripts/product-source-hash.sh` needed `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` for the
   `windows-2025` runner's bash step.
2. `minicon.com` outgrew the 9 MiB Candidate ceiling from `mux`+`harness`; the
   owner raised it to 11 MiB by explicit dated decision (see "Artifact
   budget" in `prd/PRD_02_27_con_delivery.md`) rather than trimming 0.2.0's
   scope.

This repo also vendors redacted copies of the `run-reputation-and-release`,
`sign-macos-artifacts` and `sign-windows-artifacts` skills under
`.claude/skills/` as a direct result of this release, so a future cloud/
Linux-only agent does not get stuck not knowing how to sign/release (see
`AGENTS.md`'s "Skills: look them up BEFORE acting").
