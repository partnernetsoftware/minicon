# v0.2.25 release history

## User-visible result and claim boundary

[v0.2.25](https://github.com/partnernetsoftware/minicon/releases/tag/v0.2.25)
is public. The window has a fixed `@0` Home destination outside real tab IDs,
separate New Terminal and New Web buttons, wider terminal and visible composer
scrollbars, and a clearer account Login/Service route summary before sign-in or
reconnect. The robot and Settings remain separate destinations. Behavior and
open acceptance belong to [Home](../PRD_02_39_home_dashboard.md),
[browser controls](../PRD_02_25_02_browser_controls.md),
[workspace](../PRD_02_25_con_workspace.md) and
[Harness experience](../PRD_02_31_01_01_harness_experience.md).

The owner authorized publication of this version. The release does not claim a
real OpenCode/Google or DeepSeek grant, an account-scoped native authorization
proxy trace, a paid model call, or a complete external-client tool journey.
Those cases remain open in the Harness and provider owners. The separate
[0.2.26 @0 canvas plan](../../plan/plan-v0.2.26.md) is a design target, not a
visual feature in these bytes.

## Retained failures and evidence limits

[40467193836](https://github.com/partnernetsoftware/minicon/actions/runs/40467193836)
passed its standard gate but failed an obsolete smoke driver that still waited
for the removed New menu. Its unsigned pack
[40467141223](https://github.com/partnernetsoftware/minicon/actions/runs/40467141223)
was cancelled when the driver changed. The second development attempt
[40469284083](https://github.com/partnernetsoftware/minicon/actions/runs/40469284083)
passed its standard, native browser and native auth gates, then failed two
selected tests that still described the New menu and the old 12-pixel scrollbar.
Its matching unsigned pack
[40469262915](https://github.com/partnernetsoftware/minicon/actions/runs/40469262915)
was cancelled. The exact later source above executed and passed the corrected
subjects; earlier failures remain failed historical runs.

Native test-build authorization contexts and synthetic account/proxy fixtures
prove their named isolation and no-direct-fallback mechanisms only. No usable
real provider account was available on the local release host, and the
independent native account-login proxy court still lists that request trace as
unclaimed. External client health, real provider balances and live task actors
are not inferred from a running listener or cached account. Native physical
input, every OS/ISA viewport/language and future office animation retain their
own PRD acceptance scopes.
