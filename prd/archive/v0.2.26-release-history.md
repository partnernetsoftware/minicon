## User-visible cut and limits

The public release adds a default-list @0 watch floor with exact tab routes,
attention and scoped local status; an inspectable Visual preview; zero-tab
convergence; and independently created Web tabs. The Windows native courts
cover named slices. Other platform, scale, real provider and typed inside-tab
agent-run scenarios remain open under [Home canvas](../PRD_02_39_01_home_canvas.md),
[workspace](../PRD_02_25_con_workspace.md),
[browser lifecycle](../PRD_02_28_01_browser_context_lifecycle.md) and
[Harness workspace](../PRD_02_36_harness_workspace.md).
Home reports Unknown where no accepted observer exists. Visual cards do not
claim drag placement or office characters in this cut.

successful public publish.

agent stages, Visual drag, and every OS/ISA viewport and language combination
